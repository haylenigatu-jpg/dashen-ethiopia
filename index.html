Before we begin

A land of highlands, kingdoms and coffee

Ethiopia sits in the Horn of Africa. Its high plateau is split by the Great Rift Valley, and its story stretches back thousands of years — yet it is also young, fast-changing and home to more than 80 languages.

    The coffee ceremony

    Buna: coffee as a ceremony of welcome

    ቡና

    Smell the incense? In many Ethiopian homes, coffee is not a quick drink. It is roasted, ground and brewed in front of guests — and shared slowly, over conversation.

    The ceremony happening in this restaurant is the same ritual shared in homes across Ethiopia.

    What the ceremony means

    Customs vary between regions, communities and households — names, snacks, the number of rounds and the role of incense can all differ. What follows is a common pattern, not a single rulebook.
